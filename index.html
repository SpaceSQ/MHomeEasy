How a result is decided

结果怎么来的

The light uses the ecosystems you selected, the hubs you said you own, and the compatibility record shipped with the app. Compatibility notes are refreshed about once a week, and the new entries are listed in Me. When sources disagree, an official platform or CSA record outranks a lab test, which outranks a manufacturer claim, which outranks community reports. A newer price or a paid placement cannot change the light.

灯来自你选的生态系统、你说自己有的中枢，以及随应用附带的兼容记录。兼容性说明大约每周更新一次，新条目列在「我」里面。来源冲突时，官方平台或 CSA 记录优先于实验室测试，再优先于厂商说法，再优先于社区报告。更新的价格或付费展示不能改变灯。

Sign in

登录

After the opening animation, sign in with an Apple account. Sign out returns to that screen and leaves the home on this iPhone. Signing in again opens the same home. Erase deletes the home and the settings on this iPhone. The next Apple sign-in starts empty. Details are on the support page and the privacy page.

开机动画之后，使用 Apple 账户登录。登出会回到那个页面，家还留在这台 iPhone 上。再次登录打开的是同一个家。注销会删除这台 iPhone 上的房屋和设置。下次用 Apple 账户登录从空记录开始。细节在支持页和隐私页。
