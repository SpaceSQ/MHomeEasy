Sign in with Apple

使用 Apple 账户登录

MHomeEasy uses Sign in with Apple on this iPhone. There is no separate MHomeEasy account on a company server, and the app does not send the Apple credential to an MHomeEasy server.

The sign-in request asks Apple for your name and email. The app stores the Apple user identifier on this iPhone. When Apple provides a name, the app stores that name on this iPhone as well. The app does not save the email Apple may return.

Sign out keeps the home and settings on this iPhone and only requires Apple sign-in again. Erase deletes the home, settings, and saved pairing codes on this iPhone. The next sign-in starts with an empty record.

MHomeEasy 在这台 iPhone 上使用「通过 Apple 登录」。公司服务器上没有另一套 MHomeEasy 账号，应用也不会把 Apple 凭证送到 MHomeEasy 的服务器。

登录请求会向 Apple 询问姓名和邮箱。应用把 Apple 用户标识存在这台 iPhone 上。Apple 提供姓名时，应用也把姓名存在这台 iPhone 上。应用不保存 Apple 可能返回的邮箱。

登出会把房屋和设置留在这台 iPhone 上，只是需要再次使用 Apple 账户登录。注销会删除这台 iPhone 上的房屋、设置和已保存的配对码。下次登录从空记录开始。

What is stored on this iPhone

存在这台 iPhone 上的内容

The app keeps a local snapshot of:

  • Whether you are signed in, the Apple user identifier, and the name when Apple provided one.
  • The home profile: display name, ecosystems, whether you own or rent, and the hubs you marked.
  • The My Matter desk: devices you checked and the last light, plans you opened, experience answers you kept, and the shopping list.
  • The language you chose. English is the default until you switch.
  • The Do Not Sell or Share switch.
  • The count of model attempts for the current calendar day. That count is not an API key.

Pairing-code payloads are not in that snapshot.

应用在本机保存一份快照，其中包括：

  • 是否已登录、Apple 用户标识，以及 Apple 提供姓名时的姓名。
  • 家庭资料：显示名称、生态系统、自住或租房，以及你标出的中枢。
  • 「我的 Matter」台面：你查过的设备和最后一盏灯、你打开过的方案、你留下的经验答案，以及购物清单。
  • 你选的语言。在你切换之前，默认是英文。
  • 「不要出售或分享」开关。
  • 当天的模型尝试次数。这个次数不是 API 密钥。

配对码原文不在这份快照里。

Pairing codes

配对码

Matter pairing codes you choose to save go into the Keychain. By default the item stays on this device. If you turn on saving in iCloud Keychain, the item can sync through your own iCloud Keychain. MHomeEasy has no upload path for those codes. Erase deletes the pairing codes stored for MHomeEasy on this iPhone. A shared home summary leaves the codes out.

你选择保存的 Matter 配对码进入钥匙串。默认只留在这台设备上。如果打开存入 iCloud 钥匙串，条目可以通过你自己的 iCloud 钥匙串同步。MHomeEasy 没有上传这些配对码的路径。注销会删除 MHomeEasy 存在这台 iPhone 上的配对码。分享的家庭摘要不包含配对码。

Camera, local network, and HomeKit

相机、本地网络和 HomeKit

Barcode, Matter QR, and on-box text use the camera through VisionKit on the device. The scan is a compatibility check. The app does not upload the camera frame and does not save it as a photo library item.

The optional Thread check uses the local network to look for Thread border routers by Bonjour. It does not join a Thread network and does not control a device.

When you ask the app to check Apple Home for a hub, it reads a coarse summary: whether a home hub is present, and counts of accessory categories. Accessory identifiers, rooms, and home location are not copied into the saved home profile. HomeKit data is not sent to ads or analytics, and it is not passed into affiliate parameters.

条码、Matter 二维码和盒子上的文字通过设备上的 VisionKit 使用相机。扫描是一次兼容检查。应用不上传相机画面，也不把它存成相册照片。

可选的 Thread 检查使用本地网络，通过 Bonjour 查找 Thread 边界路由器。它不加入 Thread 网络，也不控制设备。

当你让应用向苹果家庭查询中枢时，它读取一份粗略摘要：是否已有家庭中枢，以及配件类别的数量。配件标识、房间和家庭位置不会复制进已保存的家庭资料。HomeKit 数据不会送到广告或分析，也不会进入联盟营销参数。

Purchases and paid links

购买和付费链接

Subscriptions are Apple In-App Purchases. Apple processes the payment. MHomeEasy receives the subscription status needed to unlock Plus or Pro on this iPhone. A retailer link on the shopping list can be a paid link. That label is a commission disclosure. It does not change the compatibility light. This website does not run a checkout.

订阅是 Apple 应用内购买。付款由 Apple 处理。MHomeEasy 收到在这台 iPhone 上解锁 Plus 或 Pro 所需的订阅状态。购物清单上的零售商链接可以是付费链接。那个标记是佣金披露。它不会改变兼容灯。本站没有结账。

Do not sell or share

不要出售或分享

MHomeEasy does not sell your personal information. There is no advertising or analytics SDK, and the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Me includes the switch “Do not sell or share my personal information.” The switch lives on this device. While it is on, the app does not add sale or share flags to links that leave the app. Turning the switch on does not send a request to a data broker. The home profile, the My Matter desk, pairing codes, the language choice, the Apple user identifier, HomeKit category counts, and camera scans are not sold.

MHomeEasy 不出售你的个人信息。没有广告或分析 SDK，应用也不会跨其他公司的应用或网站跟踪你。

「我」里面有开关「不要出售或分享我的个人信息」。开关留在这台设备上。打开时，应用不会给离开应用的链接加上出售或分享标记。打开开关不会向数据经纪商发送请求。家庭资料、「我的 Matter」台面、配对码、语言选择、Apple 用户标识、HomeKit 类别数量和相机扫描都不会出售。

Questions you send

你发出的问题

The help form lets you write a title, role, name, optional organization, contact email, and message. Submit opens your own Mail app, addressed to xiangmiles@gmail.com. The app does not transmit that form. If you send the mail, the message then reaches that mailbox. This website stores the language you pick for these pages in local storage on your browser. It does not use analytics cookies.

帮助表单可以填写标题、身份、姓名、选填的机构、联系邮箱和内容。提交会打开你自己的邮件应用，收件人是 xiangmiles@gmail.com。应用不传输这张表。如果你把邮件发出去，内容才会到达那个邮箱。本站把你为这些页面选择的语言存在浏览器的本地存储里。它不使用分析 Cookie。

Language model

语言模型

The current iPhone app has no live model client and no API key. Verdicts, the safety card, barcode and Matter recognition, product lookup, the Thread check, the Keychain, help search, and My Matter do not call a model. Ask from saved experience answers on the device when the bundled library matches. Free cannot use a model. Plus and Pro are allowed 30 attempts a day, and only when saved experience does not match.

当前的 iPhone 应用没有已经接上的模型客户端，也没有 API 密钥。判定、安全卡、条码和 Matter 识别、产品查找、Thread 检查、钥匙串、帮助搜索和「我的 Matter」都不会调用模型。已保存的经验对上时，「从已保存经验里问」在设备上回答。免费方案不能使用模型。Plus 和 Pro 每天可以有 30 次尝试，而且只有已保存的经验对不上时才可以。
