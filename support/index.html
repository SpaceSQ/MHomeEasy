Sign in, sign out, and erase

登录、登出和注销

  1. After the opening animation, the app waits on Sign in with Apple.
  2. Sign out, from Me, returns to that screen immediately. It does not delete the home, checks, list, language, privacy switch, or pairing codes on this iPhone. The next sign-in opens the same record.
  3. Erase, from Me, deletes the home, checks, list, pairing codes, and settings on this iPhone, then returns to Sign in with Apple. The next sign-in starts empty and walks through the 90-second setup. Erase does not cancel an App Store subscription.
  1. 开机动画之后，应用停在「使用 Apple 账户登录」。
  2. 在「我」里登出，会马上回到那个页面。它不删除这台 iPhone 上的房屋、检查记录、清单、语言、隐私开关或配对码。下次登录打开的是同一份记录。
  3. 在「我」里注销，会删除这台 iPhone 上的房屋、检查记录、清单、配对码和设置，然后回到 Apple 登录。下次登录从空记录开始，并走一遍 90 秒设置。注销不会取消 App Store 订阅。

How to use the app

怎么用

  1. The first time, after Apple sign-in, walk through the apps you use, whether you own or rent, the speaker or TV box already in the home, then one sample device. You get a light before any purchase. To do the setup again, open Me and choose Redo the 90-second setup.
  2. A circle means Works. A triangle means Works with a catch. A square means Does not work with this home. A dashed circle means Not enough evidence. The word sits next to the shape.
  3. Open Check and scan the barcode, or type a barcode, Matter code, or model. The light is a compatibility result for your home. Scanning does not buy anything.
  4. Open Check and choose Devices keep dropping off for the Thread network check. That screen looks for the boxes that connect Thread devices. It is optional. The app does not control those routers.
  5. Open Solutions and choose Ask from saved experience. A local match stays on this phone and does not use a model attempt. Model answers are part of Plus and Pro, only when nothing saved fits, up to 30 times a day. Free does not include model answers. The current app does not contain a live model client, so no model request is sent.
  6. The electrical safety card is free on every plan. It tells you to stop and hire a licensed electrician for in-wall mains or gas work. It does not tell you which wire to touch.
  7. After setup, the next launch opens My Matter when you are still signed in. Pairing codes stay in the Keychain and are not listed as secrets on that desk.
  8. Open Me to switch language. English is the default until you switch. The choice is saved on this phone.
  9. On a result, choose Save to my list. Saving does not change the light. A Paid link badge is a disclosure. It is not a compatibility fact.
  1. 第一次用 Apple 账户登录后，走完设置：你用的应用、自住还是租房、家里已经有的音箱或电视盒，然后试一个设备。买之前就能看到灯。想再做一次，打开「我」，选「重做 90 秒设置」。
  2. 圆形表示能用。三角形表示能用，但有条件。方形表示和这个家不匹配。虚线圆表示证据不够。文字就在形状旁边。
  3. 打开「检查」，扫描条码，或输入条码、Matter 码或型号。灯是针对你家的兼容结果。扫描不会购买任何东西。
  4. 打开「检查」，选「设备老是掉线」，查看 Thread 网络。那个页面查找用来连接 Thread 设备的盒子。这一步是可选的。应用不控制那些路由器。
  5. 打开「方案」，选「从已保存经验里问」。本机对上的答案留在这台手机上，不占用模型次数。模型回答属于 Plus 和 Pro，只有已保存的经验对不上时才用，每天最多 30 次。免费方案不包含模型回答。当前应用没有已经接上的模型客户端，所以不会发出模型请求。
  6. 电气安全卡在每个方案里都免费。遇到入墙电源或燃气，它让你停下来，请持证电工。它不会告诉你碰哪根线。
  7. 设置完成后，只要仍处于登录状态，下次打开会先进入「我的 Matter」。配对码留在钥匙串里，不会把密钥摊在这个台面上。
  8. 打开「我」切换语言。默认是英文，在你切换之前一直是英文。选择会存在这台手机上。
  9. 在结果页选「保存到我的清单」。保存不会改变灯。Paid link 标记是披露，不是兼容结论。

A path for review

审核可以这样走

  1. Open MHomeEasy. After the animation, sign in with Apple. English is showing until you change it in Me.
  2. Finish the setup and read the first light. The words are Works, Works with a catch, Does not work with this home, or Not enough evidence.
  3. Open My Matter, Solutions, Check, My Home, List, and Me once. On iPhone, List and Me may be under More.
  4. In Check, type a model if the camera is unavailable. On iPhone, Scan the barcode uses the on-device camera.
  5. Open Me → Privacy and read Do not sell or share my personal information. The switch stays on this device.
  6. Open Me → Plans. Free still shows the main light. Plus is $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year. Pro is $9.99 a month or $79.99 a year. The safety card stays free.
  7. Open a safety card on a mains-level result. It refuses installation guidance and does not show a wiring diagram.
  8. Open Me → Sign out or erase. Sign out returns to Apple sign-in and keeps the home. Do not use Erase unless you intend to delete the local record.
  1. 打开 MHomeEasy。动画之后使用 Apple 账户登录。在「我」里改语言之前，界面是英文。
  2. 做完设置，读第一盏灯。文字是「能用」「能用，但有条件」「和这个家不匹配」或「证据不够」。
  3. 打开「我的 Matter」「方案」「检查」「我的家」「清单」和「我」各一次。在 iPhone 上，「清单」和「我」可能在「更多」里。
  4. 在「检查」里，如果相机不可用，就输入型号。在 iPhone 上，「扫描条码」使用本机相机。
  5. 打开「我」→ 隐私，阅读「不要出售或分享我的个人信息」。开关留在这台设备上。
  6. 打开「我」→ 方案。免费方案仍然显示主灯。Plus 是每月 $4.99 或每年 $39.99。Pro 是每月 $9.99 或每年 $79.99。安全卡仍然免费。
  7. 在强电级别的结果上打开安全卡。它拒绝提供安装指导，也不显示接线图。
  8. 打开「我」→ 登出或注销。登出会回到 Apple 登录并保留这个家。除非有意删除本地记录，否则不要点注销。

If something does not respond

没有反应时

  • The home setup appears only after Apple sign-in, and only when this iPhone has no finished home. Sign out does not send you there.
  • A gray dashed circle means the local record does not have enough evidence. That is a result, not a stalled screen.
  • Camera scanning runs on iPhone. If the camera is unavailable, type the barcode, Matter code, or the model printed on the box.
  • Ask from saved experience shows the local answer when one matches. If nothing saved fits, Free explains that model answers are part of Plus and Pro. The current app does not send a model request.
  • Thread merge notes name Apple Home, Google Home, or SmartThings. MHomeEasy does not send the merge itself.
  • 房屋设定只在 Apple 登录之后出现，而且只在这台 iPhone 上还没有完成的家时出现。登出不会把你送进设定。
  • 灰色虚线圆表示本地记录证据不够。这是一个结果，不是页面卡住。
  • 相机扫描在 iPhone 上。如果相机不可用，请输入条码、Matter 码，或盒子上印的型号。
  • 「从已保存经验里问」对上了就显示本机答案。对不上时，免费方案会说明模型回答属于 Plus 和 Pro。当前应用不会发出模型请求。
  • Thread 合并说明会点名苹果家庭、Google Home 或 SmartThings。MHomeEasy 自己不发送这次合并。

Plans

订阅

Free: one home, the main light and the one-sentence reason, and the safety card. Free cannot use the language model.

Plus: $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year. Per-ecosystem breakdowns, Thread merge notes, and the Multi-Admin setup guide. 30 model attempts a day, only when saved experience does not match.

Pro: $9.99 a month or $79.99 a year. More than one home, bulk checks, and CSV or PDF handoff. The same 30 model attempts a day.

The safety card is free on every plan. A paid plan does not change the light. Apple bills the subscription. Cancel it in the App Store.

免费：一个家、主灯和一句话原因，以及安全卡。免费方案不能使用语言模型。

Plus：每月 $4.99，或每年 $39.99。跨生态明细、Thread 合并说明，以及 Multi-Admin 设置指南。每天 30 次模型尝试，只有已保存的经验对不上时才用。

Pro：每月 $9.99，或每年 $79.99。多个家、批量检查，以及 CSV 或 PDF 交接。同样是每天 30 次模型尝试。

电气安全卡在每个方案里都免费。付费不会改变灯。订阅由 Apple 扣款。在 App Store 里取消。
